# Credits

Flow's agents and skills took rules and adapted text from three MIT-licensed projects.

## superpowers

By Jesse Vincent. Taken: the plan shape (exact paths, small tasks, no placeholders) from `writing-plans`; the status contract (DONE, DONE_WITH_CONCERNS, NEEDS_CONTEXT, BLOCKED), escalation, self-review, the rule that an executor never dispatches a reviewer, the two-verdict task review, distrust of the executor's report, the severity scale, the named-risk rule for looking beyond the diff, the "cannot verify" rule, the file-growing rule and clean test output from `subagent-driven-development` (its `implementer-prompt` and `task-reviewer-prompt`). Also from `brainstorming`, adapted into `skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md`: asking what the user wants and why before building, one open question per message, playing back in chunks with guesses marked, sizing the work, the written description and the builder check (from its `builder-check-prompt`).
